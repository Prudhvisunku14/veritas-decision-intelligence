# Business Policies

- Regional managers may view only their assigned region.
- Marketing managers may review marketing-related operational metrics but not restricted finance details.
- Company-wide pricing changes require Commercial leadership approval.
- Inventory reallocation should be proposed to the Supply Chain owner.
- Low-confidence evidence must be stated explicitly and should not be converted into a definitive root-cause claim.
- Analyst corrections are stored immediately but applied only after validation and scheduled recalibration.
